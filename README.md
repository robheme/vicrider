**Introducing VicRider: Your Study Companion for the Victorian Motorcycle Learner Knowledge Test**

Built with Gemini, VicRider is a new web tool designed to help prospective riders in Victoria, Australia prepare for and pass their Motorcycle Learner Permit Knowledge Test.

**Key Features:**

* **Comprehensive Question Bank:** Includes 150 built-in practice questions sourced directly from the *VicRoads Rider Handbook* and general Victorian road rules.
* **Customisable Study Sessions:** Set the exact number of questions you want to tackle per session.
* **Topic Filtering:** Focus your study on the *Rider Handbook*, general Victorian road rules, or a mix of both.
* **Smart Question Selection:** Choose between pure random selection or prioritising unseen questions to ensure full coverage of the material.
* **AI-Powered Question Refresh:** Generate fresh practice questions on demand using your own Google Gemini API key (stored locally and securely in your browser).
* **Instant Scoring & Detailed Reviews:** Get immediate feedback on your performance with thorough explanations for every answer.
